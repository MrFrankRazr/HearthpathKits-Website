Our story

Making life at home easier to organize.

Thoughtful planning tools for people who want their important household information in one manageable place.

Made for everyday life.

At Hearthpath Kits, we believe practical organization should feel approachable. Our downloadable worksheets are designed to help households record, find, and maintain essential reference information.

We are starting with family emergency readiness, and plan to explore caregiver information organizers, moving planners, and household management tools.
